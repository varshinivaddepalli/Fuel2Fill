## The Problem We Solve

**Section Header:**
Running fuel stations shouldn't mean drowning in paperwork

**Body:**
You own the stations. You hire the people. You serve thousands of customers every day. But instead of focusing on growth, you're stuck reconciling meter readings, chasing down attendance records, and hunting through filing cabinets for last month's invoices.

The data exists. It's just scattered across spreadsheets, paper logs, WhatsApp messages, and the memories of your station managers.

---

## Our Solution

**Section Header:**
One dashboard. Every station. Complete visibility.

**Body:**
Petro Astra centralizes your fuel station operations into a single, intelligent platform:

**Infrastructure at a glance**
Register and monitor every station, tank, pump, and nozzle. Know your capacity, track your stock, and spot issues before they become problems.

**Employee management that works**
Assign shifts, track attendance, and manage your workforce across all locations. No more phone calls to check who showed up.

**Sales you can trust**
Record daily meter readings, track fuel prices, and break down payments by cash, UPI, card, and credit. Discrepancies surface automatically.

**Credit customers under control**
Manage credit limits, track outstanding balances, and maintain vehicle registries. Know exactly who owes what.

---

## The AI Advantage

**Section Header:**
Ask questions. Get answers.

**Body:**
**Ask Astra** turns your business data into instant insights. Type a question in plain language:

- "What were total diesel sales last week at my Pune station?"
- "Which employees worked the most overtime this month?"
- "Show me credit customers with balances over ₹50,000"

Get charts, tables, and numbers—not database queries.

**Click Astra** reads your documents so you don't have to. Upload invoices, receipts, or delivery challans. Our OCR extracts the data you need and organizes it for easy verification and export.

# Petro Astra V1 - Marketing Video Prompt

## Flow Video Creation Prompt

**Project:** Petro Astra V1 - Hero Section Video
**Duration:** 30 seconds
**Style:** Black & white, corporate yet approachable, modern tech aesthetic
**Format:** Scrolling/parallax effect for website hero

---

## VISUAL DIRECTION

Create a sleek, monochromatic video using only black, white, and grayscale tones. The aesthetic should feel like premium SaaS—clean lines, subtle shadows, smooth transitions. Think Apple meets enterprise software. Motion should be fluid and confident, never rushed.

---

## SCENE BREAKDOWN (30 seconds)

### [0-3s] OPENING - The Problem
- Visual: Abstract fuel station silhouette fading in from darkness
- Multiple scattered paper documents, spreadsheets, receipts floating chaotically
- Text overlay: "Managing fuel stations shouldn't feel like this."
- Transition: Documents compress and dissolve

### [3-8s] SCENE 1 - Unified Dashboard
- Visual: Clean dashboard interface emerging from the chaos
- Show: Station cards, employee counts, real-time stats
- Multiple station icons connecting with subtle animated lines
- Text overlay: "One platform. Every station."
- Micro-animations: Numbers counting up, status indicators pulsing

### [8-14s] SCENE 2 - Ask Astra (AI Analytics)
- Visual: Chat interface with typing animation
- Show: Natural language query appearing: "What were my total sales last week?"
- Response: Clean chart/visualization materializing
- Text overlay: "Ask anything. Get instant answers."
- Effect: Subtle AI sparkle/glow effect (in white) around the response

### [14-19s] SCENE 3 - Click Astra (OCR)
- Visual: Invoice/receipt image being uploaded
- Transformation: Document scans, data fields auto-populate
- Show: Extracted data appearing in clean table format
- Text overlay: "Scan documents. Extract data automatically."
- Effect: Scanning line sweeping across document

### [19-24s] SCENE 4 - Operations Montage
- Quick cuts (1.5s each):
  - Employee shift calendar with assignments
  - Fuel price chart with trend line
  - Credit customer balance cards
- Text overlay: "Employees. Prices. Credit. All in sync."
- Transition: Elements merge into unified view

### [24-30s] CLOSING - Tagline & CTA
- Visual: All interface elements gracefully minimize into the Petro Astra logo
- Logo: Clean, centered, breathing animation
- Tagline fades in below: **"Ask. Analyze. Accelerate."**
- Final text: "Start your free trial" with subtle underline animation
- Hold for 2 seconds on final frame

---

## TYPOGRAPHY

- Headlines: Bold, sans-serif (similar to Inter or SF Pro Display)
- Body text: Clean, medium weight
- All text in pure white against dark backgrounds OR black against light panels
- Text should fade/slide in smoothly, never pop abruptly

---

## MOTION PRINCIPLES

- Easing: Smooth ease-in-out on all transitions
- Parallax: Subtle depth layers moving at different speeds for scroll effect
- UI animations: Interface elements should feel alive—hover states, micro-interactions
- Pacing: Confident and measured, not frantic. Let each scene breathe.

---

## AUDIO DIRECTION (if adding later)

- Ambient, minimal electronic track
- Subtle UI sound effects (soft clicks, whooshes)
- Build to gentle crescendo at tagline reveal

---

## KEY MESSAGING HIERARCHY

1. **Problem:** Chaos of manual fuel station management
2. **Solution:** One intelligent platform
3. **Differentiator:** AI-powered (Ask Astra + Click Astra)
4. **Proof:** Comprehensive operations coverage
5. **Action:** Tagline + CTA

---

## DO NOT INCLUDE

- Color (strictly B&W/grayscale)
- Voiceover narration
- Stock footage of generic people
- Cluttered screens or overwhelming data
- Fast, jarring transitions

---

## BRAND DETAILS

**Tagline:** "Ask. Analyze. Accelerate."

**Target Audience:** Fuel station owners and enterprise chains seeking modern, AI-powered management solutions.

---

## COMPETITOR DIFFERENTIATION

Unlike traditional petrol pump software (SOFTGUN, Quicksoft, PumpOne, Petro Genius) that focus only on billing and inventory, Petro Astra offers:

- **Ask Astra:** Natural language AI queries - ask questions in plain English, get instant analytics
- **Click Astra:** AI-powered OCR document processing - scan invoices, extract data automatically
- **Modern UI:** Next.js with instant navigation and real-time updates
- **Multi-station:** Manage all your stations from one dashboard
